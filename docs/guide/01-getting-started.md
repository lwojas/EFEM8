# Getting started

FM.8 is a groovebox that runs in your web browser. Open it, tap **START**, and a beat is already playing. Everything
in this guide is a way of turning that beat into your own.

## Meet the machine

FM.8 has eight tracks and one shared effects section. **Each track plays one of eight FM instruments, and you choose which.**
Every sound is built from FM synthesis, but you never see operators or algorithms: each instrument has just five knobs,
tuned so the whole turn of every knob sounds good.

| Instrument | What it is | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| **KICK** | A kick with a pitch sweep | drum |
| **SNARE** | A snare that can blend into a clap | drum |
| **HAT** | A metallic hi-hat | drum |
| **TOM** | A tuned, drum-skin tom | drum |
| **BASS** | A punchy two-part FM bass | melodic |
| **PLUCK** | A short, plucked tone | melodic |
| **BELL** | A ringing, inharmonic bell | melodic |
| **LEAD** | A lively, unstable lead | melodic |

A new project starts with the eight in that order, one per track. Change any track in **SOUND** by tapping another
instrument under **ENGINE**. Drum instruments get big pads. Melodic ones get a keyboard and an arpeggiator.

If you put two HATs on different tracks, they cut each other off, the way an open and closed hat do on a real kit.

## Finding your way around

FM.8 fits itself to your screen. On a phone, the controls are split across tabs. On a computer or tablet, nearly
everything is on one page. The controls are the same either way.

**On a phone**, five tabs run along the bottom:

| Tab | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **SEQ** | The track strip, step pads, keyboard, the effects you've recorded, and the punch pads |
| **SOUND** | The instrument choices, the knobs, the track's filter, and its delay and reverb |
| **MIX** | Level, mute and solo for all eight tracks |
| **FX** | Each track's delay and reverb, plus the Punch FX |
| **PTN** | Your pattern slots, copy, clear and chain |

**On a computer or large tablet**, everything sits on one page. The play controls and pattern buttons run across the top.
Under them is a band with the instrument knobs, the track effects and the Punch FX. The sequencer fills the rest of the
screen, with a slim mixer beside it. Drum instruments show big pads; melodic instruments show a two-octave keyboard.

If your window is short (for example, when FM.8 is running as a plugin inside your music software), the delay and
reverb knobs move to a strip under the main knobs so the keyboard still fits on screen.

## The play controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **PLAY / STOP** | Starts and stops the beat. On a computer keyboard, press `Space`. |
| **REC** | Records the notes you play on the keyboard onto the steps while the beat is playing. See [Sequencing](02-sequencing.md#recording-notes-as-you-play). |
| **BPM** | `−` and `+` change the tempo one beat per minute at a time, from 60 to 200. |
| **PTN / STEP** | Shows which pattern is playing and which step you're on. Tap it to open the pattern list. |
| **SWING** | Pushes every second 16th note a little late for a looser feel. 50% is perfectly straight; the knob goes up to 75%. Double-tap to reset. |
| **SAVE / LOAD / ☰** | Saving, loading, key and scale, display options and MIDI. See [Projects](07-projects.md). |

## Using the controls

- **Knobs**: drag up/down or left/right to turn them. **Double-tap** a knob to put it back to its starting value. If a
  knob is selected, the arrow keys nudge it.
- **Sliders** work the same way, and a double-tap resets them too.
- **Clear buttons** (anything that says CLR) ask you to tap twice, so one accidental tap never wipes your work.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Space` | Play / stop |
| `1` – `8` | Select a track and hear it |
| `[` `]` | Previous / next page of steps |

## Your first five minutes

1. **Tap START.** The demo beat starts playing.
2. **Mute and solo.** Press **M** on the kick to drop it out, then press it again to bring it back. Press **S** on the
   bass to hear it on its own.
3. **Change an instrument.** Select a track, open **SOUND** (or look at the band across the top on a larger screen) and
   pick another instrument under **ENGINE**. Try **BELL** on the kick's track. Your rhythm stays; the sound changes.
4. **Move a knob.** Select **BASS**, then turn its knobs and the track's **CUT** and **RES** while the beat plays.
5. **Punch something in.** Hold the **DRIVE** or **SWEEP** pad, then let go. Now press **REC FX**, hold a pad while the
   pattern loops, and your move is recorded and plays back every time round.
6. **Save.** Give your project a name with **SAVE**. FM.8 also saves for you as you go, so if you close the page
   and come back, you'll pick up where you left off.
